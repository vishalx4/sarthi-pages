Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy describes exactly what Sarthi collects, why it is collected, who it is shared with, and how to have it removed. It covers the Sarthi website and its booking service.

What we collect

Who What
Anyone booking a session Name, email address, phone number, and the date and time of the session booked.
Guides (mentor applicants) Full name, email address, phone number, a hashed password, and a short "about me". Plus UPSC exam credentials submitted for verification: roll number, exam year, category, optional subject, CSAT and paper status, Prelims/Mains/Interview scores, cut-off marks and qualification status.
Everyone Technical records created by using the site: IP address, browser user agent, and session timestamps. These exist to keep accounts secure and to detect abuse.

Uploaded scorecards are not kept. A guide may upload a Prelims or Mains scorecard instead of typing every field by hand. The file is read on our own server to extract those fields and is not stored — only the extracted values are saved. The document is never sent to any third-party OCR or document service.

Why we collect it

  • To run bookings. A session needs a name, a contact address and a time.
  • To verify guides. Exam credentials are the entire basis of the promise Sarthi makes to aspirants. They are checked against the guide's claims before a listing goes live.
  • To create the meeting. Each confirmed booking creates a calendar event with a video link, and invites both people.
  • To sign you in. One-time passcodes are sent to your email address or phone number.
  • To keep accounts secure. IP address and user agent are recorded with sessions.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Who it is shared with

Sarthi shares the minimum necessary with two service providers:

Provider What they receive, and why
Google When a booking is confirmed, we create a Google Calendar event with Google Meet enabled. The email addresses of the guide and the aspirant are sent to Google as attendees, along with the session title, description and time, and Google emails the invitation to both. Handled under Google's own privacy policy.
MSG91 Delivers one-time passcodes. Receives the email address or phone number the code is being sent to, and nothing else.

Other than that, personal data is disclosed only where the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

Where it is stored, and for how long

Data is held in our own database. Booking records and guide credentials are retained while the account is active and for as long as needed to resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Session records are short-lived and expire automatically. Ask us to delete your data and we will, except where we are required to keep a record.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct anything wrong, or delete it. You can withdraw consent for the processing described here at any time — though we may then be unable to provide the service. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Users in India have these rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; users elsewhere may have equivalent rights under their own law. We apply the same process to everyone.

Children

Sarthi is intended for candidates preparing for the UPSC Civil Services Examination and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects how your data is used, tell you directly.
