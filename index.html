What Sarthi does

Most aspirants fail not for lack of dedication, but because nobody is reading their answers and naming their specific gaps. Sarthi exists to fix that one thing: it matches an aspirant with a guide by journey, not only by rank — someone who failed Prelims three times before clearing, or who prepared while working full-time, or who rewrote their Mains approach and succeeded.

Guides apply, submit their UPSC credentials for verification, publish the sessions they offer, and set their own availability. Aspirants browse, pick a stage that matches their situation, and book a single session. Each confirmed booking creates a Google Calendar event with a Google Meet link for both people.

How guides are verified

Every guide submits their exam credentials — roll number, exam year, and their Prelims, Mains or Interview scorecard — before they can be listed. Sarthi checks those against what the guide claims. There are no "ex-faculty" listings and no unverified rank-holders.
